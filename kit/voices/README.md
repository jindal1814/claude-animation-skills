# Narrator voice

`narrator_ref.wav` is the reference clip the default narrator (Chatterbox Turbo) copies its voice from. It is a
synthetic voice, Kokoro's "am_adam" at speed 1.25 (Apache 2.0), not a recording of any person. Chatterbox copies its pace too, so a brisk clip gives a brisk read. Replace it with another clip of 7 to
15 seconds of clean speech to change the narrator; only use a voice you have the right to use, never someone's
recording without their consent.
